# Music references

| Material | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Well-mastered acoustic / classical | Reveals over-compression and excess reverb |
| Modern pop/EDM streaming masters | Reveals exciter harshness and bass mud |
| Jazz trio / vocal jazz | Center image and cymbal air |
| Hip-hop with deep subs | Bass enhancer vs definition of kick |
| Legacy rock CDs (pre-loudness war) | Dynamics headroom for autogain |

**Playlist tip:** keep a “neutral”, “bass heavy”, and “bright vocal” trio for every music preset revision.

**Preset focus:** `music-hd-01`, `music-hd-02`, `classic-music-01`.
