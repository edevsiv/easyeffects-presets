| Parameter | Before | After |

| bass_amount | 20.0 | 6.0 |
| bass_blend | 30.0 | 10.0 |
| stereo_base | 15.0 | 5.0 |
| exciter_amount | 18.0 | 8.0 |

Improved primary category metrics without removing the preset’s role (light vs heavy / enhancer character where intentional).
