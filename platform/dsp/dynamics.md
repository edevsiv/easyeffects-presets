# How to preserve dynamic range

## Intent

“Louder” ≠ better. Cinema and music benefit from contrast.

## Practices

1. Prefer gentle multiband ratios over brickwall broadband compression
2. Keep Autogain target conservative
3. Leave limiter as ceiling, not constant GR
4. For classical / acoustic, avoid heavy enhancer stacks

## Checks

Score **Dynamic Range** on listening forms; compare vs Flat in A/B protocol.
