# How to use Convolver

Convolver applies an impulse response (IR) for headphone correction, crossfeed-like
effects, or room/HRTF experiments.

## Practices

- Match IR sample rate to PipeWire (often 48 kHz)
- Measure added latency before competitive gaming use
- Prefer documented open IRs ([impulse-responses/](../../impulse-responses/))
- Do not vendor proprietary IRs
