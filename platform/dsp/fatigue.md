# How to avoid listening fatigue

Fatigue is a first-class metric in [measurements/METRICS.md](../../measurements/METRICS.md)
and certification gates (V5).

## Common causes in EE presets

1. Excess high-shelf / exciter harmonics
2. Constant limiter gain reduction
3. Over-wide stereo (phasey highs)
4. Long sessions on bright gaming EQ

## Mitigations

- Prefer `*-01` for long sessions
- Cap session time when testing `*-02` / experimental
- Lower exciter / treble before raising volume
- Take breaks; re-score Fatigue on the listening form

## Platform rule

A profile cannot reach **Validated** if Fatigue < 3.0 without an explicit short-session waiver.
