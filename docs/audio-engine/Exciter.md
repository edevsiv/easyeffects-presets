# Exciter

Part of the [audio engine handbook](README.md).

## Function

Generates high-frequency harmonics to increase brilliance and “clarity”.

## Advantages

- Restores dull streams/laptop rolloff
- Maps well to FxSound “Fidelity”

## Disadvantages

- Sibilance and fatigue
- Emphasizes poor encodes (streaming artefacts)

## When to use

Music HD, experimental enhancer stacks, dark headphones.

## When to avoid

Bright IEMs; speech-only presets (prefer EQ + de-esser).

## Practical examples

- Very low mix values first
- Pair with de-esser if vocals get sharp
